Use case: precise-object-edit / identity-preserve. Create TWO production cutout SHOE attachments for a gentle HEEL RAISE, based EXACTLY on reference images 1 and 2, which are the neutral screen-left and screen-right shoes of a chibi maid character. Preserve exact navy blue Mary Jane shoes, white instep opening, navy strap, small round gold buttons, thick blue sole and matching 2D anime shading.
CRITICAL: keep FRONT VIEW and exact facing direction of the reference. BOTH toes face directly toward the viewer, NO outward turn, NO side view, NO diagonal shoes, NO left/right tilt, NO yaw rotation. Heel rise is subtle front-view foreshortening: forefoot/toe stays centered and on a horizontal invisible floor, ankle rises straight up slightly. Toe center must remain directly below ankle center, no sideways displacement. Sole at front becomes very slightly thinner, shoe top a little steeper, heel rises BEHIND and is mostly hidden from this front view. Do NOT invent prominent side heel or pointy high heels. Same chibi round toe silhouette/width as neutral.
Include a SHORT straight WHITE SOCK/ankle extension above each shoe so the ankle joins the calf cleanly, no frilled upper sock cuff, NO skin, NO knee, no calf. White sock sides must be straight, narrow and symmetric, no bulge or detached stripes. Only extend about one half shoe-height above the strap. Exactly one strap and one instep opening per shoe, matching references.
Layout: TWO isolated shoes side by side, equal scale and same sole baseline, left attachment in LEFT HALF and right attachment in RIGHT HALF; big transparent gap between, ample margins. TRUE transparent background. NO labels, no text, no shadow, no scene, no extra footwear, no assembled character. High resolution crisp clean cutouts, solid opaque color interiors, antialiased edges. This is redraw of subtle raised-heel pose, not turn feet outward.
